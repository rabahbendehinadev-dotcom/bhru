==================================================
3. DEFINE TOTAL SPENT CORRECTLY
==================================================

Current Total Spent includes charges for orders that may still be pending/processing.

Change reporting semantics:

Total Spent =
sum of service charges for COMPLETED service orders only.

Rejected/refunded orders must not contribute to Total Spent.

Pending/processing orders must not contribute to completed Total Spent.

Do not mutate historical ledger rows.

Use authoritative order + financial snapshot data.

4. ADD NET SERVICE CHARGES
==================================================

Where useful in reseller Financial reporting, support:

Net Service Charges

Meaning:
gross service-order debits
minus exact service-order refunds.

This is different from Total Spent.

Do not show this metric everywhere unless it improves clarity.
