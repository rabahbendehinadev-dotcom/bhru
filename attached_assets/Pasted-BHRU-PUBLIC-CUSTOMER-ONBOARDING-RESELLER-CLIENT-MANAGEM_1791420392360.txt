==================================================
CURRENT BUSINESS PROBLEM
==================================================

When a visitor registers successfully on a reseller's public website:

- authentication works
- customer account exists
- My Account works

BUT:

The registered customer does NOT automatically appear as a real client inside the reseller's Clients/Suppliers management.

The current E-Commerce Customers implementation is mainly based on guest/order contact information and is NOT a proper registered-client management system.

This must be fixed.

DESIRED BUSINESS MODEL:

Public customer registers
→ becomes a real reseller client
→ immediately appears in Clients/Suppliers
→ reseller can open the client profile
→ reseller sees client identity, profile, WhatsApp, location, account status, financial summary and orders
→ no order is required before the client appears.

A newly registered client with zero orders must still exist in the reseller panel.
